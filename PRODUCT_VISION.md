# ElderEase AI — Product Vision

## Vision

Create a simple and compassionate AI companion that helps older adults express emotions and take small meaningful steps toward connection and wellbeing.

## Target User

Older adults who may want a simple, approachable way to express how they are feeling.

## Core Problem

Emotional wellbeing tools can be complicated, impersonal, or difficult to navigate.

## Product Principle

**Keep it simple. Keep it human.**

## Core Journey

1. User expresses how they feel.
2. ElderEase understands the emotional context.
3. ElderEase responds with empathy.
4. ElderEase suggests one relevant activity.
5. ElderEase remembers the interaction.

## Design Principles

- Simple
- Accessible
- Calm
- Respectful
- Conversational
- Privacy-conscious
- Safety-aware

## What ElderEase AI Is NOT

- Not a medical diagnostic system
- Not a therapist
- Not an emergency service
- Not a replacement for human relationships

## Success Criteria

A user should be able to open ElderEase and understand what to do without needing instructions.
