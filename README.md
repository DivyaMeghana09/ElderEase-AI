# 💙 ElderEase AI

### A gentle AI companion for everyday emotional wellbeing

ElderEase AI is a simple and accessible companion designed to help older adults express how they are feeling, receive supportive responses, and engage in small meaningful activities.

## 🌱 Problem

Older adults may sometimes experience loneliness, sadness, anxiety, or tiredness. Many digital applications can be complicated or difficult to navigate.

ElderEase AI aims to provide a simple and approachable experience where users can express their feelings and receive gentle support.

## 💡 Core Experience

**Talk → Understand → Support → Suggest → Remember**

1. The user expresses how they are feeling.
2. ElderEase identifies the expressed mood.
3. ElderEase provides a supportive response.
4. ElderEase suggests a simple meaningful activity.
5. The interaction and mood can be reflected in the user's mood history.

## ✨ Current Features

- 😊 Mood selection
- 💬 Natural text-based emotional expression
- 🧠 Basic mood detection
- 💙 Supportive responses
- 🎵 Personalized activity suggestions
- 📅 Mood history
- 🛡️ Safety-aware messaging
- 👴 Simple and accessible interface for older adults
